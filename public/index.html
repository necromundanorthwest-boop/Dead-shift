Survivor

Complete console sequences at the four critical systems. Each needs one cycle per player, with 28 seconds of commissioning between cycles. Personal work orders add 12 seconds to the station clock.

Infiltrator

Blend in, perform real or fake work, and use Blackout or Air Failure. Attack a nearby worker when the cooldown ends. Hatches connect Generator and Evacuation.

Hazards & evidence

Air Failure contaminates Sump, Ventilation, and Cargo. Blackout energizes the marked generator floor patch. Exposure kills at 100%; leave the hazard to recover. Inspecting a body reveals symptoms, never a culprit.

Discussion & escape

Use the central alarm once per worker, or report a nearby body. 45 seconds to discuss, 20 to vote. A strict majority is required to eject. Roles are confirmed on ejection. After all repairs, the lift arrives in 75 seconds and boards for 15; at least two living Survivors must stand inside when it closes.
