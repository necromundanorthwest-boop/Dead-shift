# Design overview

DEAD SHIFT is an original private-room game for 4–6 workers in Relay Station 17. Exactly one worker is secretly hostile. Social information is the primary game; continuous movement creates observations and absences.

The pillars are cause-versus-culprit uncertainty, authoritative hidden information, clear station conditions, and a physical evacuation finale. A body may result from an attack, an energized generator patch, or toxic air. Inspection reveals symptoms, never ownership of sabotage.

The loop is lobby → private role briefing → movement/repairs/incidents → discussion/voting → continued repairs → lift arrival/boarding → result. Survivor victory requires ejecting/eliminating the Infiltrator or extracting at least two living Survivors. The Infiltrator wins if fewer than two Survivors remain, integrity expires, or evacuation closes without two Survivors.

Scope: one nine-room map, two roles, two sabotages, four critical systems, three support consoles, seven themed sequence templates, private rooms, meeting-only text, external voice. Training is a distinct solo exploration mode with no hostile and a one-person escape requirement. No accounts, public matchmaking, progression, built-in voice, or persistence.
