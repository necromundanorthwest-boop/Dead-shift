# Initial balance model — unvalidated

| Quantity | 4 players | 5 players | 6 players |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Infiltrators | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Total critical cycles | 16 | 20 | 24 |
| Attack cooldown / initial delay | 90s | 75s | 65s |
| Sabotage cooldown | 65s | 65s | 65s |
| Evacuation quorum | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Active integrity budget | 720s | 720s | 720s |

One task needs at least six seconds. Commissioning adds 28 seconds between cycles at a console. With ideal parallel repair and no interference, the fastest system completion is roughly 108/142/176 seconds, before travel, followed by 90 seconds of evacuation. Therefore this alpha can finish materially faster than the brief's 10–15 minute target. Human discussion, investigation, sabotage and travel are expected to extend it, but that is not evidence of achieved duration.

A plausible—not measured—contested timeline is briefing at 0:00; dispersed first repairs by 0:30; initial sabotage around 1:00; first incident/review around 2:00; renewed work and second review around 4:00; final repair around 6:00; arrival/boarding around 7:30. Longer discussions and inefficient routes can extend the shift. Do not advertise a validated 10–15 minute duration yet.

Playtest at 4, 5 and 6 players. Record total duration, time to first incident, ejection accuracy, completion time, grouping, escape rate, hostile win rate and eliminated waiting time. Tune repair counts/commissioning only after observing those distributions. No hidden rubber-banding or difficulty adaptation is implemented.
